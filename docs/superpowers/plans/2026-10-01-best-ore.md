# Best ore to mine, by where it's found — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The Mining tab ranks every ore and ice type by what it earns, grouped by where in New Eden it is found, so
choosing what to mine stops being a matter of trying ores one by one in Scaling up's Ore picker.

**Architecture:** A pure data module says where each ore is found (from sourced research, its source named); a pure
function ranks priced ores within each place; a panel on the Mining tab prices every ore's plain grade at Jita (the page's
own three-way pricing, `priceOres`), shows ISK a m³ and ISK an hour, opens a row to its higher grades, and a click sends
an ore to Scaling up's Ore picker.

**Tech Stack:** React + TypeScript + Vite, ESI (live Jita books), the bundled SDE type materials; tests in
`scripts/check.mjs` (Node type stripping) and `scripts/pages.mjs` (Playwright, headless).

**Spec:** the user's request and their three choices, 30 September and 1 October 2026 (memory `mining-best-ore-later`):
"The mining side hustle needs a part that tells me what the most profitable ore is to farm for the different ore
categories." Chosen on 1 October 2026 from three offered options each:
- **Category = where it's found**: "High-sec, low-sec, null-sec, Pochven, moons, ice. Answers 'what should I mine where I
  am'. ESI doesn't say where ores spawn, so this comes from EVE University's tables, and the page names that source."
- **Measure = both** "ISK an hour — For the ship and fit picked in Scaling up, at the chosen character's skills, with ISK
  per m³ shown beside it" and "ISK per m³ — What a cubic metre of ore sells for: the best of selling it raw, compressed or
  reprocessed, after tax."
- **Grades = "Best grade, others inside"**: "One row per ore at its plain grade, opening to its higher grades. Higher
  grades pay more but are rarer in a belt, so the plain grade is the fair comparison."

The research is `.playwright-mcp/research/ore-locations/` (gitignored; `ore-locations.json` and `report.md`, read
1 October 2026). Read `report.md` whole before Task 1: its "Disagreements" and "Caveats for the app" sections are binding.

## Global Constraints

- Every figure read, derived or sourced, never invented (app-conventions). Where an ore is found is research: the panel
  names EVE University's Asteroids and ore page (and the ice and moon pages) with the date read, says the page itself
  asks for a post-Catalyst update, and marks the six ores the sources disagree on ("conflicting" in the JSON) as such in
  their tip, never resolving the disagreement by guess.
- An ore is priced exactly as Scaling up prices it: `priceOres` (lib/orePricing.ts), the best of raw, compressed and
  reprocessed at the main's skills and standing at Jita 4-4, after tax (the ore's worth stays the main's whoever mines
  it, as on the Characters page and in Scaling up).
- Nothing not known reads as a zero: an ore not priced yet says "Pricing…", one that failed says so with Try again, an
  ISK an hour with no pace to work from is "–" with why.
- Copy follows app-conventions: a lead line and three or four points for an intro, tips laid out (`tip={'…'}` for
  structured ones), no "collecting data" wording, ISK through `isk()` / `iskBig()`.
- Every page works at 390 px (`npm run check-phone`); a wide table scrolls inside its own box.
- Pure rules in `src/lib/` (no React, no DOM, no `./config`), I/O in components or `orePricing.ts`.
- Grep the ESI names you rely on: ESI names Scordite 0-Grade with a trailing space (trim names); moon ore groups hold all
  four ores of a rarity (match by base name, as `gradesOf` in Mining.tsx does).

## Review Focus

1. ESI failing mid-pricing (the daily downtime, 10:55–11:30 UTC): every row must say what it knows, never a zero or a
   stuck "Pricing…"; Try again re-prices only what failed.
2. A character whose pilot is an alt (Scaling up's "Show for") or whose skills aren't read: ISK an hour must follow the
   pace Scaling up shows for that character, and say whose.
3. Ice against ore: an ore fit can't mine ice and an ice fit can't mine ore; Mercoxit needs deep-core lasers. A row the
   chosen fit can't mine must say so, not show a pace it doesn't have.
4. Pricing ~60 types plus their compressed forms and the minerals must not take minutes or hammer ESI: shared bid cache,
   limited concurrency, plain grades first, higher grades only when a row opens.
5. 390 px: the ranking table, its opened grade rows and the place selector must not stick out past the screen's edge.

### Task 1: Where each ore is found, and the ranking rule (pure)

**Files:**
- Create: `src/lib/oreWhere.ts`
- Test: `scripts/check.mjs` (a new `--- best ore by where it's found ---` block)
- Modify: `.claude/rules/mining.md` and `.claude/rules/eve-ships.md` (`paths:` gain `src/lib/oreWhere.ts`)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `type Place = 'highsec' | 'lowsec' | 'nullsec' | 'pochven' | 'wormhole' | 'moon' | 'ice'` and `PLACES: { key: Place; label: string }[]` in display order (High-sec, Low-sec, Null-sec, Pochven, Wormholes, Moons, Ice).
  - `type Found = { place: Place; how: 'belt' | 'anomaly' | 'rare' | 'sov' | 'drill' | 'ice belt'; where: string }` — `where` is short copy ("Amarr and Caldari space, 0.9 to 0.5"; "Rare anomalies in 0.5 systems bordering low-sec"; "Sov upgrade: Isogen Prospecting Array").
  - `ORE_WHERE: Record<string, { kind: 'asteroid' | 'moon' | 'ice'; found: Found[]; disputed?: string }>` keyed by base name (every ore in `FAMILIES` in miningFits.ts, plus every ice type in the research), `disputed` carrying what the sources disagree on, in a sentence.
  - `ORE_WHERE_SOURCE: { name: string; url: string; read: string; caveat: string }[]` (EVE University's Asteroids and ore, Ice harvesting and Moon mining pages; CCP's Catalyst and 23.01 notes), read 1 October 2026.
  - `rankOres(rows: OreRow[], place: Place): OreRow[]` where `OreRow = { base: string; iskPerM3: number | null; iskPerHour: number | null }`: the rows found in `place`, ranked by ISK an hour where every row has one, else by ISK a m³; unpriced rows last, in name order. Deterministic ties by name.
- Consumes: `FAMILIES`, `oreBase` from `src/lib/miningFits.ts`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `scripts/check.mjs`: every base ore in `FAMILIES` has an `ORE_WHERE` entry with at least one place; every `found` place is a key of `PLACES`; Veldspar is a high-sec belt ore, Kernite in high-sec only as `rare` (Catalyst's 0.5–0.8 anomalies) and as a low-sec belt; Spodumain is Pochven only; the four ubiquitous moon ores and Gelidus and Krystallos carry `disputed`; `rankOres` orders by ISK an hour, falls back to ISK a m³ when any row lacks a pace, puts unpriced rows last by name, and leaves out an ore not found in the place.

Keep `where` short (it shows in a table cell); the longer note can go in a `detail` field shown in the row's tip.
